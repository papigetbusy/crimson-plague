# Changelog (newest first, one plain line each)
- 2026-10-21: A room now holds five players (you and four friends); monsters scale up for the extra players and the fifth assassin gets its own colour. The lobby now shows "N of 5 players" and the host button says up to 5.
- 2026-10-21: A room now holds four players (you and three friends); monsters scale up for the fourth player and the fourth assassin gets its own colour.
- 2026-10-21: Fixed picking up guns: the gun from your hands now drops beside you (with the ammo it had) instead of under the gun you reached for, and with several guns on the floor pick up takes the one you are looking at, so you can carry two.
- 2026-10-21: Friends' hands now hold only the gun they really carry (the plain stand-in of their gun type); the pistols, glowing hands and witch staff are gone. Assassins come in three colours (shadow black, blood red, deep teal), one per player slot.
- 2026-10-21: Friends are now drawn as the class they picked (Gunslinger: wide hat and long coat with two pistols; Assassin: hood, mask and glowing white hands; Witch: tall pointed hat, robe and a green orb staff), placeholders until Grok's models arrive; and the start card now fits a sideways phone (page width fixed, compact layout, scrolls if the room panel is open).
- 2026-10-21: Play with friends (first test, up to 3 players): PLAY WITH FRIENDS on the start card makes a 5 letter room code; the host's game runs the monsters, waves and boss for everyone, and each friend keeps their own gun, loot, gold, and level-up picks. Monsters get tougher and more numerous with more players, a downed friend is brought back by a teammate standing close for 4 seconds, and the run ends when everyone is down. Friends are drawn as simple placeholder bodies with name tags and a party health list.
- 2026-10-20: Three new bosses from Crusader Tactics: the Bat Lord (circles, then dives through where you stood and drains your health), the Lich King (soul-bolt fans, a ring of bolts with gaps, blinks away, raises the dead) and the Elder Dragon (sweeping fire cone, tail-slam shockwave); one of the four bosses is picked at random for each boss floor from the map seed (never the same twice running), glowing floor warnings show each big attack coming, and every boss gets more health and damage on each later floor (not pushed).
- 2026-10-20: The guide arrow under the wave counter is now a sharper arrowhead with a gold edge, and the minimap (corner and big) draws a gold trail along the real walking route from you to the Demon Lord while he guards the door (not pushed).
- 2026-10-20: Fixed: holding fire through a reload no longer gives an infinite fire rate burst afterwards; tabbing out during a level-up no longer hides the RESUME button (soft lock); a refused mouse capture after tabbing back no longer turns mouse look off for good; aim and shoot buttons held together work. New: every gun type on the floor has its own default model (pistol, SMG, rifle, shotgun, sniper with scope, heavy machine gun) in its rarity colour.
- 2026-10-20: Fixed on PC: holding right-click to aim and then left-clicking to shoot (or the other way round) only registered the first button, because the browser sends a press event for the first button only; both buttons are now read together. Added to the mouse check.
- 2026-10-20: Fixed on PC: the mouse stayed captured during a level-up so you could not click a card; it is now released while a level-up or the shop is open and taken back after. The number pad keys 1-6 pick level-up cards too.
- 2026-10-20: The start card now shows a small build number (the short git id) so testers can say exactly which version they are on.
- 2026-10-20: Fixed: a level-up that arrived while you stood at the merchant (after a boss) could not be picked with 1/2/3 or a tap because the shop took the input; the level-up now always comes first and the shop returns after it. New never-stuck tests.
- 2026-10-20: Seeds and saves: you save only by pressing Save game at the stash chest in a safe room, never automatically (newest 8, deletable, Continue from the start or game over card); a save keeps the map seed so you get the same floors, while the damage and item seeds are rolled fresh each time you enter, so you can keep going back to the same map for better luck.
- 2026-10-20: The stash is now a chest by the merchant (walk up, press pick up to open; it says stashed things cannot be used until you fall), blueprints start a run with one gun plus one of each gear piece, and the Gear menu, stash menu, start card and game over card all work with a controller (D-pad moves, A presses, RB/Back closes).
- 2026-10-20: Gun mods (own lines, 1/1/2/2/3 places by gun rarity, fit only within one rarity of the gun, bag of 6, fit and remove in the Gear menu) and a first stash: put things away in a safe room, keep them as blueprints (3 per type) when a run ends, and start runs with them. The stash is open from the start for now; the unlock rules come later.
- 2026-10-20: Roadmap: consumables moved to Phase 9; gun mod rules written down (own affixes, 1 per common and 2 per uncommon, a mod must be within one rarity of its gun, drops follow the gun wave bands). Not built yet.
- 2026-10-20: Gear on the floor is now picked up on purpose too (tap its card, E, or A), so nothing is ever taken by accident; hearts and flames still pick up by walking over them.
- 2026-10-20: Codex on the start card (and pause): how to play, classes, monsters, guns, rarities, status effects, affixes, gear and the merchant, built from the game's own numbers, with search. Merchant now sells two pieces of gear. Shot effects (muzzle flash, streak, hits, kills) follow the gun in your hands: rarity sets the strength, status colours set the look, legendaries alternate two colours; poison is purple, dark magic black-violet, light magic white.
- 2026-10-20: Guns on the floor are no longer picked up by walking over them (so a good gun is never swapped away by accident in a fight): tap the floating card, press E, or press A on the controller. The gun card now floats above the gun itself instead of sitting over the health bar.
- 2026-10-20: Gear: armor (head, chest, boots) and two charms drop from monsters (bosses always), with affixes that fit the piece and class-only lines; they raise defense, health, speed, damage and more. A GEAR button (key G, controller RB) opens a menu with both guns and everything you wear. Also: the gun box pops up for 2 seconds then fades, and the cheat build is titled and tagged CHEAT BUILD.
- 2026-10-20: Gun affixes like Crusader Tactics (common 0-1, uncommon 1-2, rare 2-3, epic 3, legendary 3-4; class-only lines only drop for that class) and a small card for the gun on the floor showing its numbers, status and affixes against your own gun.
- 2026-10-20: Touch: Slide moved clear of the map, new Swap button above Run (dim with one gun), Triangle swaps on controller, Q shown on the start card and gun box.
- 2026-10-20: Shotguns reload shell by shell (hand goes to the belt three times, then racks the pump) instead of swapping a magazine (not pushed). Snipers also work the bolt after every magazine.
