# Credits
Guns: 20 catalog guns are built from the Quaternius Ultimate Gun Pack (CC0, https://quaternius.com) and shaped by tools/mesh_to_gun.js; Kenney Blaster Kit (CC0) was downloaded too but not used yet.

Crimson Plague uses free art packs the owner picked up online. The effect sheets and the wall stone are packed into `src/screen/fx_data.js` and `src/screen/wall_data.js` (made by `tools/pack_fx.py` and `tools/pack_walls.py`). The original full pack files stay on the owner's side and are not in this repository.

- **Super Pixel Effects Gigapack** by Will Tice / unTied Games: bullet, muzzle, blood, smoke and explosion effects.
- **Tiny Swords** by Pixel Frog: the wall stone (menu pieces to come).
- **Raven Fantasy Icons**: icons for the menus (not used yet).
- **PeerJS** (MIT licence): the WebRTC link for playing with friends, loaded from a CDN, with its free public matchmaking server.

Thank you to the artists. Owner decision (2026-10-19): this is a private game for the owner and one friend; credit is given here.
